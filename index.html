About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Accountancy at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research focuses on financial reporting and disclosure, examining how information flows between investors, analysts, and managers. I am also interested in AI-related settings and applying AI-based methods in accounting research.

As a research assistant for Professor Narissa Brown, I completed a replication paper on non-GAAP research and am currently exploring new settings including managerial learning, analyst use of prediction markets like Polymarket, and the effects of AI-generated financial text on analyst interpretation.
